Welcome to Wealth OS! 👋

This is your completely private, offline personal finance dashboard. None of your data ever leaves your device.

To get started: Create a profile below and import your portfolio holdings file. We will automatically analyze, categorize, and project the future value of your entire portfolio!

🤖 Auto-Pilot Engine Room

Wealth OS runs on a 100% Auto-Pilot algorithm. It constantly analyzes your portfolio's expected future wealth and automatically maps the optimal assets to your goals based on financial best practices and time horizons. No manual tracking required.

Short-Term (0-3 Yrs): Favors Safe/Cash assets (FD, RD, Savings, Debt)
Mid-Term (4-7 Yrs): Favors Balanced Funds, Debt, Gold
Long-Term (7+ Yrs): Favors Stock Market, Real Estate, Retirement Funds (EPF/PPF)

Profile Data & Management

Create asset owner profiles and upload portfolio files directly into them. We seamlessly support automated data extraction from INDMoney, Zerodha (Kite & Console), Upstox, Scripbox, Groww, ICICI FD, and Post Office statements.

Install Application

Install Wealth OS on your home screen for quick, offline access.

Backup & Restore

Export your entire portfolio, custom profiles, goals, and manual overrides into a single JSON file. Use this file to restore your data or transfer it to another device.

Danger Zone

Permanently delete all profiles, investments, goals, and settings from this device. Make sure you have downloaded a backup first! This action cannot be undone.
